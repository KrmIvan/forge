FORGE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

Controller: KRM Engine Studio (the owner's other details are on FORGE's Google Play listing).

Privacy contact: krmenginestudio@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

  • Training data: exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes, routines, records and progress. Stored only on your phone.
  • Optional profile data: display name, photo, height, birth year and body weight, only if you add them. Stored on your phone.
  • If you create an account: username, display name and an encrypted password; if you sign in with Google, also your Google account's email, identifier and name. The server only receives the date of each finished workout (no exercises or weights), for your Crew's stats and achievements.
  • Profile photo: private by default and it never leaves your phone. Only if you turn on "Profile photo visible to your Crew" is it uploaded so your Crew can see it; making it private again deletes it from the server.
  • Crews: crew name and emblem, members, crew achievements, who has trained this week and the fires you give.
  • Crew chat: the messages you send, and the reports and blocks you make or receive.
  • Purchases: whether you are premium, which plan and until when, and the purchase history Google Play provides through RevenueCat, linked to your account or to an anonymous identifier. Google Play handles payment: we never see your card or bank details.
  • Promo codes: which code you redeem, when, and attempts in the last 48 hours (to limit them to 3 every 24 hours).
  • Notifications: the rest countdown and the daily reminder are created on your phone and send no data. If you turn on "News and offers", OneSignal receives the device's notification identifier and technical data (model, operating system, language, time zone and approximate country from the IP); never your workouts or account.
  • We don't use advertising or third-party analytics, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service (workout log, account, Crews and chat): performance of a contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Managing purchases, premium and promo codes: performance of a contract.
  • Moderating the chat, handling reports, preventing abuse (e.g. guessing codes) and protecting users: legitimate interest in a safe service (art. 6.1.f GDPR).
  • Sending news and offers: your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), withdrawn by turning the option off in Settings › Notifications.
  • Showing your photo to your Crew: your consent, withdrawn by making it private.
  • Legal obligations (e.g. tax or requests from authorities): legal obligation (art. 6.1.c GDPR).
  • Health data you may log (body weight, notes) is processed only because you choose to add it, with your explicit consent (art. 9.2.a GDPR), and stays on your phone.

4. Who we share it with

Only with providers processing data on our behalf:

  • Supabase Inc.: database and accounts, with servers in the European Union.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (USA): management of Google Play purchases and subscriptions.
  • OneSignal Inc. (USA): sending news, only if you turn it on.
  • Google: Google sign-in and Google Play billing, under its own terms.

Your Crew members see your display name, your photo only if you make it visible, your shared activity and your messages. Nobody else sees your username, email or training data.

When a provider is outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • While you have an account. If you delete it, we erase your server data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep (e.g. purchase records).
  • Chat messages are kept while the Crew exists or until you delete them. Reports are kept up to 12 months to review abuse.
  • Promo code attempts are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Data that is only on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or from Settings.

6. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consents, by writing to krmenginestudio@gmail.com. You can also export your workouts (JSON or CSV) and delete your account in the app (Settings › Account) or at https://krmivan.github.io/forge/delete-account. If you think we mishandled your data, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

7. US users (California and other states)

In the last 12 months we collected the categories in section 2 (identifiers, commercial purchase information, content you post and device technical data) for the purposes in section 3. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive data to infer characteristics. You can ask to know, correct or delete your data by writing to krmenginestudio@gmail.com; we won't treat you differently for exercising your rights. We honor the Global Privacy Control signal, although we make no sales to opt out of.

8. Minimum age

FORGE is only for people 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect data from minors; if we learn an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it.

9. Security

The connection to the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Each user can only read their own data; Crew members only see what section 4 describes. Passwords are stored encrypted and nobody on the team can see them. No system is infallible: if we detect a breach that affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will tell you in the app before the change applies.
